# bazis-statusy 2.13.0

Requires bazis 2.16 and bazis-permit 2.11 (unchanged).

## Changes

- **The meta `for_transit` of a list** (`StatusyRouteSetBase`): the transits the user may make
  on each object of the page, by its id, `{"<pk>": ["<transit id>", ...]}` (the ids sorted,
  an object without a transit left out). They are the transits of `state_actions` of the
  item: the transit permissions whose selector and status the object matches, that start
  from its status; anonymous users get those of the roles for anonymous users. The
  validators are not called: a transit listed may still be refused with 422, and
  `state_actions` of the item keeps their `restricts`. It costs one query per condition of
  the transit permissions of the role that can give a transit from a status of the page
  (plus the page and the transits of the model), not one per object, and nothing for a
  user without a transit permission; it is computed only when the request names it
  (`?meta=for_transit`). A board shows the transits of its cards by it, instead of reading
  `state_actions` of each card.
