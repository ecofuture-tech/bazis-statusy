# bazis-statusy 2.12.0

Requires bazis 2.16 and bazis-permit 2.11.

## Breaking changes

- **The transit is closed to anonymous users.** `POST /{item_id}/transit/` is an action of
  bazis-permit declared `public=False`: an anonymous user gets 403 even with a transit
  permission of a role for anonymous users. A transit for anonymous users is a route of its
  own with `action_transit = route_declare(StatusyRouteSetBase.action_transit,
  public=True)` (bazis 2.16, see the guide); the transit then has no author (it failed with
  a 500 storing the anonymous user before).
- **`statusy.W002` is removed.** The untranslated names of the declared statuses and
  transits are listed by `manage.py bazis_messages status` of the core.
- **`StatusyMixin.statusy_children_items` is removed.** It read the children of the item
  without the computed fields of their routes; `schema_validate` reads them itself.
- **The texts of a payload are stripped.** The payload type of a transit strips the texts
  of its fields (`str_strip_whitespace`) before their constraints are checked: a required
  text (`Field(min_length=1)`, a reason of a rejection) of only spaces is refused with 422
  at its field (it was accepted, and an action that stripped it saved an empty reason),
  and the validators and the actions receive the text without the leading and trailing
  whitespace.

## Changes

- **`Workflow(initial=...)`**: the status of a new object of the model created without one
  (the API, `Model()`, `objects.create()`), instead of the global `BAZIS_STATUS_INITIAL`;
  an initial status that is not a status of the workflow is `statusy.E001`;
  `declare.initial_status(Model)` reads it (None without one).
- **The history of an item in the API**: the calculated field `status_history` of
  `StatusyMixin` (the transits of the item, the oldest first: `transit`, `status`, `dt`,
  `author`), shown by a route that names it in `include`; it is visible with the item.
- **`transit_apply` takes an instance of a payload model** as well as the dict of the body
  of the transit endpoint: it is validated as its data with the payload type of the
  transit (it failed with a pydantic `ValidationError` before: the payload type is a model
  of its own).
- **The routes of `bazis.contrib.statusy.router` are a public catalog**
  (`StatusRouteBase` and `TransitRouteBase` declare `permit_public = True`): bazis-permit no
  longer reports them with `permit.W002`. They served every user before as well, anonymous
  ones included: registering the router publishes the workflow of all the models with
  statuses. A transit no longer shows how it runs: `validators` (the names of its methods),
  `is_schema_validate` and `transits_related` are left out like `actions_before`,
  `actions_after` and `source_link`; it shows its names, hints, model and statuses.
- `GET /{item_id}/schema_transit/` is of kind `schema` in `x-bazis`.
- The declarations use the helpers of the core, `bazis.core.declare` (bazis 2.16), instead of
  those of bazis-permit.

## Fixes

- **A transit validates the children with the computed fields of their route.** The
  children of the transited item (`StatusyChildMixin`) are read with the annotations of the
  `calc_property` fields of the transit schema of their route and its filter context (the
  user: `_user`), as the route reads its objects: a restriction of the transit on a
  computed field checks the value of the API, and a `calc_property` whose method returns
  its own attribute (the recipe of the guide of the core before bazis 2.16) no longer fails
  the transit with a `RecursionError` (a 500).
- **A role that hides every field of a model validates no fields of it.** An empty list of
  fields of a schema now means "no fields" (bazis 2.16): the transit schema of a child
  whose permissions hide all its fields validates nothing of it, where it validated every
  field before.
