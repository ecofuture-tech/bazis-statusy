# bazis-statusy 2.14.0

Requires bazis 2.16 and bazis-permit 2.11 (unchanged).

## Breaking changes

- **The meta `for_transit` of a list leaves out the child transits of a `TransitRelation`.**
  In 2.13.0 it listed every transit of `allow_transits` of the object, the children of a
  `TransitRelation` too, while `state_actions` of the item shows them only nested under
  their parent: a board that offered the transits of `for_transit` offered a child alone,
  which the card offers only with its parent. `for_transit` now lists the transits of the
  top level of `state_actions` (a transit that is the child of any `TransitRelation` is left
  out, its parent is listed), so the board and the card offer the same transits. Without a
  `TransitRelation` nothing changes; the number of queries of the meta does not change
  either. The transit endpoint is unchanged: `POST /{item_id}/transit/` still takes a child
  transit the user is allowed. A client that read the children from `for_transit` reads
  them from `state_actions` of the item, nested under their parent.
