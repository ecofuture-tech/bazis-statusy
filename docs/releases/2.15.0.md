# bazis-statusy 2.15.0

Requires bazis 2.16 and bazis-permit 2.11 (unchanged).

## Fixes

- **The schemas of the body of the transit endpoint have the same names on every Python and
  on every start.** The body of a transit without a payload was named after
  `dict | None`: `Union_body_type` on Python 3.14, `DictOrNone_body_type` on 3.12 and 3.13.
  It is `DictOrNone_body_type` everywhere. The body of a transit with a payload was named
  after the ids of its payload models (`PayloadType_3478...016_body_type`, new on every
  start); it is named after the models now (`PayloadType_<model>_<model>_body_type`, the
  names sorted), and the fields of the payload (and its `required`) follow the models in
  that order. The OpenAPI and the `state_actions` of the same code are identical across
  Python versions and runs, as the contract check of bazis-front (`bazis_front contract
  --check`) requires.
