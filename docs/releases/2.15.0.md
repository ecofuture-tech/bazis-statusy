# bazis-statusy 2.15.0

Requires bazis 2.16 and bazis-permit 2.11 (unchanged). No migration.

## Fixes

- **The schemas of the body of the transit endpoint have the same names on every Python and
  on every start.** The body of a transit without a payload was named after
  `dict | None`: `Union_body_type` on Python 3.14, `DictOrNone_body_type` on 3.12 and 3.13.
  It is `DictOrNone_body_type` everywhere. The body of a transit with a payload was named
  after the ids of its payload models (`PayloadType_3478...016_body_type`, new on every
  start); it is named after the models now (`PayloadType_<model>_<model>_body_type`, the
  names sorted), and the fields of the payload (and its `required`) follow the models in
  that order. The OpenAPI and the `state_actions` of the same code are identical across
  Python versions and runs, as the contract check of bazis-front (`bazis_front contract
  --check`) requires.

## Changes

- **`Model.get_transit_payload_type(transit)`: the payload type of a transit from the model
  class**, without an object (the creation of an object computes its default status in the
  database). It is the one implementation and the one hook: the transit endpoint,
  `state_actions` and `transit_apply` call the classmethod only. `transit_payload_type`
  stays under its old name for its callers (`item.transit_payload_type(transit)` in the
  code of the projects, `Model.transit_payload_type(Model, transit)` in bazis-front 0.6)
  and returns the same; it is no hook any more.
- **`GET /{item_id}/status_history/` of `StatusyRouteSetBase`**: the history of the transits
  of the item, the oldest first, as `{"data": [...]}`; each record has the `transit`
  (`id`, `name`), `status_src`, `status_dst`, `dt`, the `author` (a resource identifier
  `{id, type}`, null for a transit of the system) and the `payload` of the transit (null
  without one). It is an action of bazis-permit with the new operation
  `StatusyAccessAction.HISTORY` (`shop.order.item.history.<selector>.<status>`), kind
  `other` in `x-bazis`: the item must be visible (404 as at its retrieve) and the user must
  have the `history` permission (403). A route set that must not give it leaves
  `action_status_history` out of its `actions`. The calculated field `status_history`
  (2.12) stays: the compact form, read with the item in one subquery under `view`; the
  route is the full, canonical form.
- **The history record keeps the source status and the payload of a transit.**
  `transit_apply` (and so the transit endpoint) writes `extra = {"status_src": ...,
  "payload": ...}` to the history record, which the actions receive: the status the
  transit started from, and the validated payload, `model_dump(mode='json',
  by_alias=True)` of the payload type, for a transit with a payload type. The fields of
  the payload models marked `NOT_IN_HISTORY` (`bazis.contrib.statusy.schemas`;
  `secret: Annotated[str | None, NOT_IN_HISTORY] = None`, or on a member of the
  `Optional`: `Annotated[str, NOT_IN_HISTORY] | None`) are validated and given to the
  methods of the transit, from a dict or from a payload model instance, but not kept. A
  mark elsewhere (an item of a list, a field of a nested model) is `ImproperlyConfigured`
  when the payload type is built, naming the field, rather than a value kept silently. The
  records made before this version have neither: `status_history` shows their source
  status by the current definition of the transit and no payload. No migration.

## Upgrading

- Starting with the first transit after the update, the history records keep the payload
  of the transit in `extra`. Before the rollout, mark every field of the payload models
  that holds personal or secret data with `NOT_IN_HISTORY`; otherwise it is stored in the
  history and read through the history route.
- **Roles get no history until the permission is granted**: grant
  `<app>.<model>.item.history.<selector>.<status>` to the roles that may read the
  payloads of the transits (and their names), which `view` of the item does not show.
- A model that overrode `transit_payload_type` moves the override to
  `get_transit_payload_type` (a classmethod).
- `create_statusy_transit(transit, author, extra=None)` takes the `extra` of the history
  record: an override of the method must accept it and pass it on.
