# bazis-statusy 2.9.0

Requires bazis-permit 2.9.0 (its declarations and helpers).

## Features

- **Statuses and transits declared in the code.** An application declares them in its
  `workflow.py` module: `WORKFLOWS` of `bazis.contrib.statusy.declare.Workflow(model,
  statuses, transits)` with `Status(id, name)` and `Transit(id, name, src, dst,
  validators=(), actions_before=(), actions_after=())`. After `migrate` (the
  `post_migrate` signal, also sent by `flush`), once all the migrations of the project are
  applied, they are in the database, in one transaction:
  - the statuses are created or renamed; they are shared by the models, so a status
    declared by several workflows has one name (else `statusy.E001`);
  - the transits of the declared models are created or updated: name, model, statuses,
    validators and actions (the other fields stay as the admin set them);
  - nothing is deleted: a status would delete its objects, a transit its history (both
    `on_delete=CASCADE`); a transit of a declared model that is not declared is reported
    by `statusy.W004`; a declared transit id that is a transit of another model stops
    `migrate`;
  - applied again, nothing is written; the cached content types (`ContentType`,
    `StatusyContentType`) are forgotten first, as `flush` makes them again with other ids;
  - the names are English msgids (`gettext_lazy`) translated into `name_en` and `name_ru`
    by the catalogs of the project.

  `apply_declarations(using, workflows=None, dry_run=False)` applies them from the code,
  or lists the changes.
- **Checks.** `statusy.E001` (model, ids, statuses of the transits, duplicates, names of
  the shared statuses), `statusy.E002` (validators and actions are methods of the model)
  and `statusy.E003` (the transit permissions declared in `roles.py` of bazis-permit name
  a declared transit and its source status) stop `migrate` and fail `bazis_doctor`;
  `statusy.W002` lists the names without a translation. With a database (`manage.py check
  --database default`): `statusy.W003`, the database differs from the declarations, and
  `statusy.W004`. They are warnings because `migrate` runs the database checks before it
  applies the declarations; `bazis_doctor` does not run the database checks.

## Upgrade

The data migrations of the workflows of a product stay as history; to move them to
`workflow.py`, declare the same ids: the first `migrate` updates them.
